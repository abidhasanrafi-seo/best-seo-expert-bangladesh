Keyword Research & Content Optimization

Find the Right Keywords, Write Content That Ranks

Data-driven keyword strategy mapped to real buyer intent

Search intent first, keywords second

Ranking isn't about stuffing keywords — it's about matching search intent. I classify keywords into informational, navigational, commercial, and transactional intent, then map each to the right content format (blog post, landing page, product page, or service page).

My keyword research process covers competitor gap analysis, long-tail discovery, search volume & difficulty scoring, and clustering — so every page targets a clear, winnable opportunity.
